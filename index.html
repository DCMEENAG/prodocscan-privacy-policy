Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how ProDocScan handles permissions, local document processing, optional cloud backups, and user privacy controls.

1 Zero Cloud Tracking & On-Device Processing

ProDocScan operates on a privacy-first, local-first design. We do not operate private application servers to collect, store, read, or monetize your documents.

  • Document Scanning & Image Processing: Boundary detection, perspective correction, cleanup filters, and ID card stitching run locally using Google ML Kit Document Scanner, OpenCV Android SDK, and on-device TensorFlow Lite segmentation models.
  • Optical Character Recognition (OCR) & Barcode Reading: Multilingual text recognition (English, Hindi/Devanagari, Chinese, Japanese, Korean) and QR/barcode scanning run directly on your device via Google ML Kit and Tesseract OCR.
  • Offline AI Document Insights: Library summaries, word counts, keyword extraction, and document categorization run purely on-device over locally extracted OCR text without transmitting document content to external AI servers.
  • PDF Tools & Printing: Merging, splitting, compressing, watermarking, rotating, reordering, locking/decrypting PDFs, and native system printing occur locally on your device.

2 Device Permissions & Why They Are Requested

ProDocScan requests only the minimum Android permissions required for core scanner and optional backup functionality:

Permission / Access Purpose Data Transmission
Camera (android.permission.CAMERA) Real-time capture of physical documents, ID cards, books, and QR/barcodes. Processed strictly locally in memory; camera frames are never recorded or sent to developer servers.
Internet (android.permission.INTERNET) Used only for user-initiated Google Drive / Dropbox backup, on-demand Tesseract OCR language pack downloads, and Play Services ML Kit model setup. Strict HTTPS/TLS only (cleartext HTTP is disabled app-wide; only system trusted CAs accepted).
Vibrate (android.permission.VIBRATE) Subtle haptic feedback during camera capture and scanner interactions. None (device hardware feedback only).
Scoped Storage & SAF (DocumentFile, FileProvider, DocumentsProvider) Saving scanned PDFs/images, importing files selected by you, creating offline .zip backups, and sharing/printing files. Files stay in app-private storage or folders explicitly selected by you. No broad external storage access is requested.

3 Optional Cloud Sync & Google API Limited Use Disclosure

Cloud backup and restore are 100% optional. If you choose not to connect a cloud account, ProDocScan works completely offline for scanning, OCR, and PDF management.

  • Google Drive Integration: Uses official Google Sign-In and requests only the narrow https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file scope. This restricts ProDocScan exclusively to files and backup folders created by ProDocScan itself—the app cannot browse, read, or modify unrelated files in your Google Drive.
  • Dropbox Integration: Uses official Dropbox OAuth 2.0 with PKCE (prodocscan://dropbox-auth) in Chrome Custom Tabs / system browser. Documents are synced directly between your device and your personal Dropbox account.
  • Account Metadata Stored Locally: When you sign in to a cloud provider, only your display name, email address, profile photo URL, and sync folder identifier are stored locally in encrypted preferences so the app can display your connected account and perform sync. We do not receive or store your account password.
Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance: ProDocScan's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Information obtained via Google Drive APIs is used strictly to provide user-requested backup and restore inside ProDocScan and is never sold, shared with advertisers, used for creditworthiness, or read by humans.

4 Security Measures & Local Data Protection

  • Hardware-Backed Token & Signature Encryption: Cloud OAuth tokens and sensitive signature bytes are encrypted at rest on your device using Android Keystore backed AES-256-GCM cryptography.
  • Encrypted Network Transport: Cleartext HTTP network traffic is blocked (usesCleartextTraffic="false") and network security rules trust system Certificate Authorities only.
  • OS Auto-Backup Excluded: Android unencrypted OS auto-backup and device-to-device transfer are explicitly disabled (allowBackup="false" and exclusion rules for local databases and files) so sensitive scans do not leak outside your explicit backup choice.

5 Optional Third-Party Downloads & Links

  • Offline OCR Language Packs: If you explicitly request additional offline Tesseract OCR languages in Settings, ProDocScan downloads the selected .traineddata model directly over HTTPS from the open-source tesseract-ocr/tessdata_fast repository.
  • Google Play Services ML Kit: Google Play Services may manage on-device ML Kit scanner/OCR module availability subject to Google Play Services terms.
  • Zero Ads & Zero Analytics: ProDocScan contains no advertising SDKs, no third-party ad tracking, and no behavioral analytics or crash telemetry trackers.

6 Data Retention, Deletion & User Control

You maintain complete ownership and control over your data at all times:

  • Edit & Delete Locally: Move documents to Trash, restore them, permanently empty Trash, or delete individual pages anytime.
  • Offline Backups: Export or restore your entire document library as a portable local .zip archive via Settings.
  • Unlink Cloud Accounts: Disconnect Google Drive or Dropbox from Cloud Sync settings anytime, or revoke ProDocScan's access directly from your Google Account Permissions or Dropbox Connected Apps settings.
  • App Uninstall: Uninstalling ProDocScan instructs Android to permanently remove the local SQLite Room database, preferences, cached renders, and app-private document files. Files previously backed up by you to Google Drive, Dropbox, or external SAF folders remain in your storage until you delete them there.

7 Children's Privacy & Policy Updates

Children's Privacy: ProDocScan is a general utility/productivity tool and does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has shared personal information via support email or cloud integrations, please contact us at testsoftwarebydc@gmail.com so we can promptly assist with deletion.

Updates to This Privacy Policy: We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app improvements or legal requirements. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date on this page.
